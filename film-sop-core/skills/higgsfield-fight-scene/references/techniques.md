## Hands

### Straight Lead (Lead Straight Punch) — The Style's #1 Strike
- Mechanics: the lead fist launches straight from guard along the centerline, without drawing back; the hip and step add mass; the fist is vertical; contact is made with the lower three knuckles; the hand immediately returns.
- On screen: a lightning “snap” without a windup; the torso barely opens.
- Prompt: `his lead right fist snaps straight down the centerline into the jaw, vertical fist, zero windup, and recoils instantly`

### Finger Jab (Bil Jee)
- Mechanics: like the straight lead, but with fingers toward the eyes/throat; even longer and faster; used as an interception.
- On screen: a “spear” hand; the opponent recoils, clutching his face.
- Prompt: `a blinding finger jab flicks toward the attacker's eyes; he flinches back, guard broken`

### Lead Hook
- Mechanics: a short hook after a straight lead, elbow no higher than the fist; usually to the jaw or liver.
- Prompt: `a tight lead hook whips into the jaw, elbow low, torso barely turning`

### Backfist (Gua Choy, Whipping Strike with the Back of the Fist)
- Mechanics: the back of the fist whips from above and to the side toward the temple/bridge of the nose, often after a trap.
- Prompt: `his hand whips over the trapped arm, backfist cracking across the temple`

### Straight Blast (Jik Chung Choy, Chain Straight Punches)
- Mechanics: a series of vertical straight punches with both hands along the centerline, maintaining forward pressure; the opponent retreats without time to recover structure.
- On screen: the hero is “carried” forward while the opponent falls backward.
- Prompt: `a chain of vertical-fist punches piston down the centerline as he surges forward, driving the man stumbling backward`

### One-Inch Punch (Only on Request — A Signature Demonstration, Not a Standard Combat Move)
- Prompt: `from an inch away, a short-range punch detonates through the chest; the man launches off his feet into the shelving`

## Legs (All Low-Line Unless Explicitly Requested Otherwise)

### Jeet Tek / Side Kick to the Knee-Shin — The Style's #1 Kick
- Mechanics: the lead leg, blade of the foot to the advancing opponent's knee or shin; often used as a stop-kick — interception at the moment the opponent steps.
- On screen: the opponent pitches forward as the leg is knocked out.
- Prompt: `as the attacker steps in, a low side kick from the lead leg spears into his knee; his leg buckles and he pitches forward`

### Oblique Kick (Jeet Tek Downward and Forward)
- Prompt: `a downward oblique kick scrapes the attacker's shin and stomps his instep`

### Hook Kick (O'ou Tek, Round Kick to the Body/Thigh)
- Prompt: `a snapping lead-leg hook kick digs into the ribs, hip barely lifting — no wind-up`

### Side Kick to the Body (Finisher)
- Mechanics: a powerful side kick to the solar plexus/ribs with a burst step; the main “launcher.”
- Prompt: `a thrusting side kick slams into the sternum, hurling the man three meters back into the crates`

## Trapping (Sticky Hands, Close Range)

### Pak Sao (Slapping-Pinning Hand)
- Mechanics: the palm pins/deflects the opponent's hand down and inward while the other hand simultaneously strikes through the open line.
- Prompt: `his left palm slaps the attacker's guard down — pak sao — while his right fist fires through the opening in the same beat`

### Lop Sao (Grabbing-Pulling Hand)
- Mechanics: grip the wrist/forearm, jerk the opponent toward you and down, and meet the pulled-in face with a strike.
- Prompt: `he seizes the wrist and yanks the man forward into a short punch — lop sao — the pull and the hit landing as one motion`

### Stop-Hit / Stop-Kick (Interception — The Style's DNA)
- Mechanics: any straight strike/kick performed AT THE MOMENT the opponent starts attacking; the opponent effectively runs onto it.
- Prompt: `the attacker cocks his fist — and eats a stop-hit straight lead mid-motion, his own punch dying halfway`

## Working Against Weapons (Knife/Stick)

- Never reach for the knife with the hands as the first movement. Distance + low kicks.
- Sequence: angle off the line → stop-kick to the knee/shin on the armed side → control the weapon arm with both hands (lop sao wrist grip) → strike/break/disarm → open the distance.
- Prompt: `he angles off the knife line, a low side kick cracking the knifeman's lead knee; both hands snare the weapon wrist — lop sao — an elbow drops onto the trapped forearm and the knife clatters away`

## Target Map (from sources/04)

JKD prioritizes “low-cost” targets that do not require strength: eyes (finger jab), groin (hook kick / low strike), knee-shin (side/oblique kick). The jaw/temple are finishing targets. A strike travels “through” the target, not merely “to” it; the fist is vertical, with its final position “in front of your own nose.”

## Opponent Reactions (Physics — Mandatory in the Prompt)

| Strike | Reaction |
|---|---|
| Straight lead to the jaw | Head snaps backward, one or two steps back, sinking down |
| Side kick to the body | Entire body flies backward, 2–3 meters, into an obstacle |
| Kick to the knee | Leg buckles; opponent pitches forward/drops to a knee |
| Finger jab to the eyes | Recoil, hands to the face, a blind pause of 1–2 sec |
| Lop sao + strike | Body is jerked forward and down into the strike, falling past the hero |
| Straight blast | Stumbles backward, arms scattered, falls onto the back |
