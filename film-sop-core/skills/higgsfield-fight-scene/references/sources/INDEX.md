# SOURCES INDEX — Victor's materials

Format: `file — [type] — contribution`

- `01-tao-of-jkd.md` — [book] Tao of Jeet Kune Do (expanded 2014 edition, complete text): the core of mature JKD, including stance, footwork, the full arsenal, stop-hit, the five ways of attack, cadence, and approximately 15 guiding quotations. PRIMARY SOURCE.
- `02-filosofia-i-duh-boytsa.md` — [book] Fighting Method, volume 5: Philosophy and the Fighter's Spirit: a master's on-screen behavior (“as in ordinary life”), anger/fear as causes of slowing, the whip principle, and Lee's camera principle of a long, static take.
- `03-fm1-priemy-samooborony.md` — [book scan] Fighting Method, volume 1. Its actual content is the training foundation: on-guard stance, approximately 15 incorrect stances usable for opponents, a footwork vocabulary, power/speed mechanics, and seven reconstructed sequences.
- `04-fm2-osnovnoy-kurs.md` — [book scan] Fighting Method, volume 2: Basic Course: footwork and strikes broken into phases, a target map, and a correct-versus-incorrect section with visual signs of telegraphing.
- `05-fm3-sovershenstvovanie.md` — [book scan] Fighting Method, volume 3: Skill Development: four answers to a jab, slips/ducks, approximately eight feint-and-strike pairs, 13 counterattack sequences, and short/tall matchups.
- `06-fm4-osvoenie-priemov.md` — [book scan] Fighting Method, volume 4. Its actual content is Self-Defense Techniques: approximately 50 self-defense sequences involving holds, chokes, sticks, knives, pistols, groups, and fighting from the ground.
- `07-metodika-borby.md` — [book scan] Method of Fighting. Its actual content is volume 4, Advanced Techniques: approximately 20 feint → reaction → strike sequences, the rule of at most two feints, the first feint covering half the distance, and three- to four-phase photographic sequences usable as storyboard templates.
- `08-dao-kung-fu.md` — [book scan] The Tao of Gung Fu (early Lee, before JKD): a 70/30 rear-weighted stance, centerline, chi sao, and a Cantonese glossary including pak sao, lop sao, jut sao, and chung chui. Useful for young-fighter scenes and traditional-school-versus-JKD scenes.

- `13-wc1-perviy-uroven.md` — [book, Liu Weihan, 2002, complete text] Wing Chun foundations: stance geometry, 13 hand positions, chain punches, chi/lap sao cycles, Siu Lim Tao in eight phases, and ten principles.
- `14-wc2-vtoroy-uroven.md` — [book scan] Wing Chun, level 2: Chum Kiu broken into sections, turns, kicks by phase, 13 chi sao exercises, 20 counters, a target-point map, and ten ready-made scene sequences.
- `15-wc-dummy-116.md` — [book scan] Wing Chun wooden dummy: the structure of the muk yan jong, 116 movements arranged into 12 patterns, combat applications, the rhythm and sound of practice (slap–tok–boom), and the Ip Man → Bruce Lee lineage as a story element.
- `16-wc-vysshiy-uroven.md` — [book scan] Advanced Wing Chun: Biu Jee with force release, elbows, combined velocities, 28 counters, and ten scene sequences.

- `17-wc-video-tutorials.md` — [12 videos, storyboards and transcripts] Wing Chun dynamics: two-phase stance entry, movement driven by the elbow, frame-by-frame block → strike conversion, dan sao cycles, street scenarios (Kevin Lee's realistic treatment versus Master Wong's cinematic treatment), two tempos (meditation ↔ a 0.3–1-second explosion), and ID-based routing.
- `video-wc/<ID>.md` — [12 files] Complete individual Wing Chun video digests.
