# JKD ALL 27 GATES (VxmkjF38FeI, 19:19)
Channel: JKD ACADEMY OF MMA Sifu George Hajnasr (16.6K)

**Essence:** A complete catalog of 27 “gates” (Gates / Fon Sao trapping hands) — basic JKD trapping combinations starting from hand contact (touching hands). A gate is a “door”/opening for interception: if the opponent blocks your strike, the hand does not withdraw but “breaks the gates” — immobilizes the blocking hand and continues striking. 27 honors Bruce Lee's birthday (November 27, 1940).
