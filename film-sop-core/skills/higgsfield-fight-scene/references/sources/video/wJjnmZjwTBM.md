# JKD - Kick Counter (Jeet Juk Tek) (wJjnmZjwTBM, 2:55)
Channel: Elite Academy of Martial Arts, GA (instructor Kevin + partner Brandon)

**Essence:** Jeet juk tek — intercepting side kick, a stop-kick with the foot to the kicking leg's shin/knee against front kick and round kick. Key: the leg is NOT chambered — simply lifted, with the foot turning; it is a “stiff-leg” — a rigid pole against which the opponent breaks his own motion. Vertical filming: gym with blue padded wall, red equipment rack, window backlight.

**Visual details for choreography**
- Stance: bai jong, strong leg forward; weight already shifted back — lead leg free for instant lift.
- Mechanics: NO wind-up/chamber — leg rises straight, foot turns flat (horizontal), knee slightly bent; torso leans back as counterweight. Visually a short “foot jab down/forward”, not a spectacular kick.
- Distance/timing: interception in FIRST third of opponent's kick — his leg is still accelerating when the foot is already on his shin. On screen: attacker seems to “step” onto an invisible stair and stop.
- Hop-in: for height difference — small forward hop of supporting foot synchronized with lifting kicking leg; 10–15 cm amplitude, not a jump.
- Scene effect: hard version — side kick through knee, opponent folds forward/falls (shown around ~96s, ~126s) — dirty, realistic finisher.
