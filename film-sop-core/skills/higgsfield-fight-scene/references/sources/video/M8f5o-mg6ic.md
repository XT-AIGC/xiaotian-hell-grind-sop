**Essence:** Seminar footage (handheld camera, gym with punching bags, Thai gym in daylight): Sifu Mark Edward Stewart demonstrates the leg cut — “cutting” the supporting/kicking leg with a step-entry INSIDE a round kick, against the instinct to “move away from the strike”. **Visual details for choreography**
- Stance: short, hands high (seminar in a Muay Thai gym — hybrid stances); instructor large and heavy, moving with a short shuffling step.
- Footwork: the core move is a diagonal burst-entry at 30–45° inside the round kick's arc as it starts; distance collapses from kicking range to clinch range in one step.
- Leg-cut mechanics: your leg/shin wedges into the supporting or kicking leg's line; the opponent does not “get hit” so much as lose structure — pitching forward/sideways. Excellent movie beat: villain throws a spectacular round kick, hero takes a tiny step inside — and the villain collapses by himself.
- Tempo: the entry must read BEFORE the kick reaches halfway around its arc — “intercepting intent”, a signature JKD beat (jeet = intercept).
- Environment: a real seminar — TOP KING bags on chains, gray mat, barefoot students in a semicircle; wandering handheld camera — usable as a found-footage-style reference.
