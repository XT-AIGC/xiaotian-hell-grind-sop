# JKD Basics: 5 Ways of Attack in Jeet Kune Do (AWHjwZdCeDg, 10:04)
Channel: fightTIPS (guest presenter Nathan Young / “Ninja Nate”), filmed in Honolulu, Hawaii.

**Essence:** Breakdown of Bruce Lee's first “5 Ways of Attack” — Simple Angular Attack (SAA) via straight lead. Video core: on-guard stance (bai jong), non-telegraphic “hand before body” strike, shuffle/push step to close distance, and a 45° exit with simultaneous strike. The other 4 attacks receive short previews at the end.

**Techniques by timestamp**
- 00:00–00:27 — Intro montage: trapping close-up (crossed arms, forearm control), sparring by yellow wall, high round kick, mitt work. Lee's “be water” quote.
- 00:57–01:45 — On-guard stance (bai jong): right foot forward at 45°, knee slightly covers groin, rear leg loaded on ball of foot “as in fencing”; hybrid boxing/fencing stance. Left (rear) hand protects centerline near throat/chest, right fist comes from ribs. Frames (strip 1, gray wall): narrow semi-frontal stance, light springing shifts, short forward/backward steps.
- 02:01–02:20 — Listing 5 ways of attack: SAA, Attack by Combination (ABC), Attack by Drawing (ABD), Hand/Foot Immobilization (HIA), Progressive Indirect Attack (PIA).
- 02:23–03:04 — Stationary straight lead, partner holds red mitt (frames 135–180 s): rear-foot pivot, “turn the toes”, power from hip and rear leg; do not lift rear heel completely or straighten knees; punch strictly along centerline, “punch down your center line”, pressure felt through to rear leg. Rear hand stays in guard throughout.
- 03:06–04:05 — Straight lead with shuffle step: rear-leg push, lead foot “thrown” forward; hand starts BEFORE or with foot — “the hand moves before anything else”, otherwise telegraphing. Frames (strips 4–5): deep lunge with widely separated legs, body weight flies forward — “all my 145–150 pounds into one fist”.
- 04:10–05:25 — Anti-telegraph drill: no wind-up/drawback; partner shows he can retreat when the hand draws back (“he's going to move away” ×4). Slow mirror work, “straight forward” repeated as a mantra; minimize any drawback toward zero.
- 05:25–06:20 — Closing distance: from realistic range (outside kicking reach), shuffle/push step, “I go past it to show I can close the gap”; two scenarios — stationary opponent (stop-hit down open line), or advancing opponent intercepted.
- 06:08–06:58 — Complete SAA: 45° step-off right + simultaneous strike, defense and attack on one beat; left hand parries in parallel. “Don't go step-step-then-punch — everything has to happen at one time”. Frames (strips 7–8): deep diagonal lunge past partner's attacking arm, punching arm fully extended at head level.
- 07:02–07:58 — Interception timing: “if I master the timing, he runs into it every single time”; partner attacks — evade + counter, free hand covers/strikes.
- 08:02–09:14 — Previews of other four (yellow wall, strips 10–12): ABC — same entry but a series; ABD — let him strike high while you go low; HIA — trap striking arm (pak sao-like) and strike, trapping both ways; PIA — first attack false, parry his hand, second strike real.
- 09:23–10:04 — Outro: “Jeet Kune Do = way of the intercepting fist”, thanks to Shane/fightTIPS.

**Visual details for choreography**
- Stance: right lead (strong hand forward), narrow, high, weight on balls of feet; torso quarter-turned, chin lowered.
- Footwork: springing shifts in place → explosive push step; SAA uses a diagonal 45° deep lunge, rear leg extending into line.
- Distance: starts outside kicking range (~2 body lengths), one impulse into punching range; strike at full extension, vertical fist to head.
- Tempo: “everything on one beat” — defense, step and strike simultaneous; no preparation/wind-up. Reads as sudden hand “shot” from stillness.
- Setting: two backgrounds — gray wall (solo technique/mitts), bright yellow wall (partner demos); both in black T-shirts, partner in shorts holding red mitt.
