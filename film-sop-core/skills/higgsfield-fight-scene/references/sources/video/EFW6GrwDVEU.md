**Essence:** Short partnered Lap Sao sensitivity drill (hand sensitivity): cyclic rolling in contact, from which a pull+strike can fire on any “beat”. Goal: remove thinking — “feel something — move” (feel pressure — react).

- Distance: very close, trapping range; forearms crossed and in contact throughout the drill.
- Stance: neutral, feet nearly together, torso vertical — deliberately “laboratory” setup, all drama in the hands.
- Movement pattern: continuous circular forearm rolling (wave-like, hypnotic) → sudden rhythm break: downward pull + punch over it.
- Tempo: steady cycle ~1 beat/sec, explosion on an unpredictable beat — excellent cinematic “meditation → flash” contrast.
- Pull short (15-25 cm), no wind-up; other hand punches on centerline toward face/chest, stops centimeters away (control).
