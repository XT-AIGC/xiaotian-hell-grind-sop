# How to Quickly Attack and Retreat with Jeet Kune Do Pendulum Step (ClfYe9Uly28, 2:29)

**Essence:** Short tutorial on pendulum step — a “pendular” foot replacement for instant attack entry and retreat, plus an intercepting kick combination (“the intercepting foot”). Filming: two people (instructor in black T-shirt, partner in red polo), empty gym with gray wall and wooden floor, static full-body camera.

- 00:15–00:34 — Pendulum-step mechanics: “move forward and you place the forward leg… move backwards and replace the back”. **Visual details for choreography**
- Stance: narrow side-on bai jong, strong leg forward, rear heel raised, hands low and relaxed (instructional, not sparring tone).
- Footwork: feet replace UNDER torso — center of mass hardly travels vertically; visually a “hop-replacement”, not a step. Screen key: feet meet in air like colliding balls.
- Distance: pendulum moves fighter exactly half a step — enough for opponent's kick to miss by 5–10 cm, then immediately regain distance with counter-kick.
- Tempo: duple “one-two” rhythm, retreat and counter blend into one beat — reads as “rebound-shot” without pause.
- Props/partner: red paddle mitts held low by thigh; holder continuously backs up — attacker “pursues” with pendulum entries.
