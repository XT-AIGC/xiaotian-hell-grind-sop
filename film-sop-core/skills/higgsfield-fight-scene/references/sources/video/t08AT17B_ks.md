# Bruce Lee JKD Workout And Isometrics (t08AT17B_ks, 12:14)
Channel: Dan Lok (with JKD instructor Octavio Quintero, student of Jerry Poteet — direct lineage from Bruce Lee)

**Essence:** Instructional video on Bruce Lee's isometric exercises: 5 exercises developing “tendon power” — explosive striking power without large muscle mass. Conversational gym demo, two presenters in Bruce Lee T-shirts, third participant (Jack) for rack exercises.

**Techniques by timestamp**
- 00:22 — Intro: why isometrics. Lee combined isometrics + weight training + endurance; isometrics provide speed and power together, grip strength and strike stabilization (two standing in medium shot, speaking to camera).
- 01:10 — Exercise 1: isometric belt/rope pull (from Jerry Poteet). Belt pinned under foot, pulling/punching at 3 positions: mid-range → almost full extension (“out here”) → mid-range again. Each held 10 seconds, counting aloud. Frames: fighter holds belt at chest height, elbow down, vertical-fist structure.
- 02:42 — Third position on shortened rope = “punching structure”, full arm extension — “that actually helps developing punching power”.
- 02:57 — Exercise 2: isometric push-ups on vertical fists (three knuckles, fists under shoulders, elbows tucked — “we don't punch like that, we punch like this”). Static holds: bottom 10-20 sec → slightly higher → mid-range → explosive rise (“explode up”). ~5 repetitions. Frames: bottom/midpoint holds, kneeling partner discussing angles.
- 04:26 — Palm variation (if fists weak), progression to fingers; emphasis on triceps engagement and tendon work, not merely a “push-up”.
- 05:17 — Exercise 3: static dumbbell hold (10-15-20 lbs) at punching height. Key: bent knees, heels “screwed” into floor, engaged core — “I'm not just standing here”. Then extend arms fully forward, hold 10-30 sec. Do not punch with dumbbells — harmful to joints.
- 07:02 — Exercise 4: isometric bar press in power rack (bar locked in). Jack beneath bar: bar on trapezius (not neck, towel optional), quads engaged, upward pressure 10 sec × 3 sets, slow lowering. Develops kicking power. Frames: three by rack, one under bar, two correcting.
- 08:56 — Exercise 5: press against fixed chest-height bar (pose from famous Bruce Lee photo). Press FORWARD, not up: heels into floor, whole body one line, deltoids + core. 20 sec × sets. Bonus — forearm conditioning against bar.
- 10:34 — Philosophy: “you're hitting with the entire body”; muscles must “memorize” pulses in different directions, adapting to any circumstance.
- 11:31 — Conclusion: Bruce was thin, “not a lot of pounds”, but hit hard through “tendon power” — tendon rather than muscle strength.
